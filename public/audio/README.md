# Audio sources

The main 21-track playlist contains the complete NetEase editions of “卯酉東海道 ～ Retrospective 53 minutes” and “大空魔術 ～ Magical Astronomy”. Audio streams through each song's public `outer/url`; album metadata and LRC are fetched during the site build. No NetEase recording is committed to this repository. Rights in those recordings and lyrics remain with their respective artists, writers, labels, and platform.

If that network path is unavailable, the player falls back to Wikimedia Commons’ MP3 transcode of “Chopin - Nocturne Op. 15 no. 2 in F sharp major”, performed by Vadim Chaimovich and released under CC0 1.0.

Source and license details: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chopin_-_Nocturne_Op._15_no._2_in_F_sharp_major.ogg>
